Refund & Cancellation Policy

Flight Tickets

1. Airline Cancellations: Fully refundable in case of total flight cancellation or schedule cancellation by the operating carrier, in accordance with global aviation regulations.

2. Client Cancellations: A 12-hour grace period applies for ticket cancellation eligibility upon direct booking, subject to specific carrier penalties and administration fees.

Consultancy & Advisory Fees

Service and administrative application processing fees are non-refundable once documentation submission has commenced with third-party bodies or embassies.
